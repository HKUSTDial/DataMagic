# Story-led presentation expansion

## Reviewed material

Read-only study of the local two 60-second economic-explainer reference excerpts, their contact sheets, and Hypit's `examples/xiaolin-clone`, `examples/complex-explainer`, and `examples/ranking-football` documentation. No reference video, real presenter image, voice, player photograph or Hypit source was copied into Cards. All three components are independently implemented in React/Remotion.

## Presentation types and priority

| Form | Narrative job | Library decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Presenter shrinks to inset while data takes the frame | Shift attention from question to evidence without restarting footage | New PresenterDataTakeover; signed trend and focal period |
| Bold claim followed by checkable decomposition | Build a contrast, then resolve it rather than swapping arbitrary title cards | New ContrastContributionStory; calculated contribution endpoint |
| Persistent tier board with moving entities | Keep previous evidence visible while revising selected objects | New PersistentTierBoard; visible fixed thresholds |
| Environmental footage + opaque perspective evidence board | Preserve contextual depth without reading numbers over busy video | Existing CharacterPerspectiveBoard / FootageEvidenceReveal; avoid a redundant new card |
| Chapter montage / keyword cutaway | Mark a thematic change before the next claim | Future candidate; needs varied own footage and measured reading time |
| Actor reactions / comic inserts | Give a pause or emphasis without pretending the insert is evidence | Future candidate; explicit generated-media provenance required |
| Word-to-graphic event alignment | Motivate animation at the spoken idea rather than arbitrary timestamps | Existing explicit beat inputs are a starting point, not automatic alignment |
| Map-to-chart handoff | Connect location, context and numerical evidence | Future candidate; validate geographic assets and layout separately |

Only the first three rows are newly runnable recipes in this batch. The last four are opportunities, not delivered templates. Existing scene examples were inspected alongside their editorial documentation; no claim of exhaustive analysis of the creator's entire channel or the full Hypit finished film is made.

## Discovery

Sidebar order: Explanation & stories → Footage & context → Camera & transitions → Chart types. Categories remain concrete forms; the Story-led explainers collection crosses them via explicit presentation metadata. Thus a waterfall story can remain a waterfall while also being discovered by narrative purpose. Each category appears in exactly one sidebar group; a card may belong to multiple purpose collections.

## Scope limits

New previews are silent, 12-second examples with synthetic data. They do not provide voice generation, lip-sync, automatic anomaly discovery, causal proof or universal long-form story compilation. Existing generated cat footage is reused. Alternate datasets exercise a different focal period, positive saving contributions, declines and two-tier jumps. Review the produced pictures before any promotional claims.
